# Discovery Review Trigger

This document serves as the formal protocol for ending the Code Freeze and initiating Version 1.1 development.

## The Trigger Mechanism
Version 1.1 development **must not commence** until all of the following gates have been successfully cleared.

1. **[ ] Discovery Exit Criteria Met**
   - Verified that thresholds defined in `DISCOVERY_EXIT_CRITERIA.md` (e.g., 50 candidate applications, 3 active recruiters) have been achieved.

2. **[ ] Quarterly Review Completed**
   - The Product Review Committee has met and filled out `QUARTERLY_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`.

3. **[ ] RFC Prioritization Finalized**
   - The `RFC_PRIORITIZATION_MATRIX.md` has been applied to all pending features, resulting in a sorted backlog.

4. **[ ] Readiness Checklist Approved**
   - The `V1_1_READINESS_CHECKLIST.md` has been fully checked off and signed by the Lead Engineer and Product Owner.

5. **[ ] Operational Health Stable**
   - The system is free of P0/P1 `BUG-###` tickets. Backups are verified, and storage targets are within acceptable limits.
