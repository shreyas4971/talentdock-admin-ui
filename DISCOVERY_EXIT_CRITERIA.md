# Discovery Exit Criteria

To avoid premature optimization and feature bloat, TalentOS must remain in Product Discovery Mode until the following operational thresholds are met. Version 1.1 development **cannot** commence until all criteria are satisfied.

## Minimum Operational Evidence
1. **Recruiter Adoption**: At least 3 active recruiters logging in and using the system weekly.
2. **Pipeline Volume**: A minimum of 50 candidates processed through the application pipeline.
3. **Position Volume**: At least 5 unique positions created and published.
4. **Time in Production**: A minimum of 4 consecutive weeks running in the production environment.
5. **Feedback Density**: At least 15 actionable feedback entries submitted via the Admin Feedback Board.

Once these criteria are met, the Product Review Committee is authorized to open the `v1.1` milestone.
