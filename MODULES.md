# TalentOS Modules

TalentOS Recruitment is a lightweight, personal applicant tracking system designed for individuals or small teams. Future development will proceed incrementally based on validated needs, without carrying unnecessary enterprise complexity.

### 1. Recruitment (Released: v1.0.0)
- **Purpose**: Manage job postings, accept candidate applications, and track candidates through the hiring pipeline.
- **Current Status**: MVP Released.

### 2. Interview Scheduling
- **Purpose**: Sync with local calendars to streamline interview booking without external calendar tools.
- **Current Status**: Planning / RFC phase.

### 3. Email Integration
- **Purpose**: Directly email candidates from the dashboard without leaving the app.
- **Current Status**: Backlog.

### 4. Personal Settings
- **Purpose**: Customize pipelines, API keys, and notification preferences.
- **Current Status**: Partial implementation.
