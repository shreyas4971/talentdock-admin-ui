# TalentOS Recruiter Quick Start Guide

Welcome to TalentOS Recruitment! This 5-minute guide covers your daily workflow as a recruiter.

## 1. Creating and Publishing a Position
1. Log in to the **Admin Dashboard** (`http://admin.talentos.local`).
2. Click **Positions** in the top navigation bar.
3. Click **+ Add Position**.
4. Fill in the **Title**, **Description**, **Department**, and **Location**.
5. Set the status to **PUBLISHED** and click **Save**.
   *(Your position is now live on the Candidates Portal!)*

## 2. Reviewing Candidates
1. Click **Candidates** in the top navigation bar.
2. By default, you'll see all applications across all positions.
3. Use the **Search bar** to quickly find a specific candidate by name or Reference ID.
4. Click on a candidate to view their details.

## 3. Downloading Resumes
1. Open a candidate's profile.
2. Locate the **Resume** section.
3. Click the download icon. The original PDF/DOCX will instantly securely download to your machine from Google Cloud.

## 4. Updating Candidate Status
1. Open a candidate's profile.
2. Review their details and resume.
3. Locate the **Status** dropdown (defaults to `APPLIED`).
4. Update the status to `SCREENING`, `INTERVIEW`, `OFFER`, or `REJECTED`.
   *(Updating to Hired or Rejected will prompt a final confirmation).*

## 5. Exporting Data to Excel
Need to share data with hiring managers?
1. Navigate to the **Candidates** list.
2. Apply any relevant filters (e.g., search by "Software Engineer").
3. Click the **Export to Excel** button in the top right.
4. A cleanly formatted `.xlsx` file will download automatically containing the currently filtered dataset.

## 6. Reporting Feedback
Something broken? Missing a crucial feature?
1. Click the 💬 **Feedback Icon** in the top right corner of any page.
2. Type exactly what went wrong or what you'd like to see changed.
3. Click **Submit**. (Your request goes straight into our engineering backlog!).
